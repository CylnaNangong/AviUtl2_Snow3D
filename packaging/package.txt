THE REAL 3D snow particle system custom object (v1.22) for AviUtl ExEdit2.
Up to 4000 snowflakes with independent 3D position, velocity, gravity, wind and
sway, drawn as true 3D geometry with natural depth occlusion. Smart spawn
volume wraps the visible frustum and pushes walls off-screen; camera-follow and
world-fixed modes, soft-edge depth of field, and lifetime fades included.
Pure script with zero plugin dependencies.
